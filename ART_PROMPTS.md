# Original Orison artwork

Created for Echo Chamber: The Drowned Choir using the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool in generation mode. No external source artwork or specific existing game's imagery was supplied. Prompts below were sent as independent generations, without reference images. Both outputs were visually inspected with `view_image` for subject, composition, readable dark title space, absence of text/UI, and appropriate palette. Pillow was used only for RGB/WebP format conversion at quality 88; artwork was not retouched or cropped.
